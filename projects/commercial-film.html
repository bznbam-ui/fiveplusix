About Commercial Film at FivePluSix

Commercial film at FivePluSix follows the same principles as every other division: the work comes first. We approach commercial briefs with the same rigor and creative ambition as our independent productions — the budget changes, the standard doesn't.

We work with brands who understand that the quality of the film they commission reflects directly on them. That means clients who trust the creative process and directors who understand the commercial context. When those two things align, the results speak for themselves.

What We Offer

End-to-end commercial production — from treatment and pre-production through shoot, post, grade, and multi-format delivery. We operate as a full-service production house for select clients, and as a creative partner for brands who arrive with an agency already in place.
