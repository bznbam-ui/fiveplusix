The Brief

An emerging artist transitioning into a new phase of their career needed a digital presence that could hold the weight of the work — something that felt established and intentional rather than provisional.

Identity System

We built a typographic-first identity system designed to work across digital, print, and physical formats from day one. The system is simple enough to be maintained without a designer and distinctive enough to be unmistakable. A full usage guide was delivered alongside the assets.

The website was built as a long-form single page with section-based navigation — prioritising immersive scrolling over conventional structure. The focus throughout was on pacing: how quickly information arrives, how much breathing room sits between ideas, what gets shown and what stays absent.

Social & Digital Toolkit

A full social asset kit was produced alongside the site — templates, motion graphics, and a colour and typography guide for day-to-day use. The artist now maintains their own digital presence within a system that was built to scale with them.
