The Brief

An original short film produced entirely in-house at FivePluSix — from script development through to grade and delivery. The project was conceived as a demonstration of the label's full-stack film capability: concept, production, and post under one roof.

Production

Shot over two days with a lean crew. The visual approach was built around practical light — no artificial sources unless unavoidable — to preserve the naturalistic texture the script demanded. The edit was locked within a week of the final shoot day.

AI-assisted tools were used in post for select VFX sequences, reducing what would have been a two-week compositing pass to three days without compromising the visual quality.

Delivery

Delivered across multiple formats — DCP for festival submission, streaming-optimised H.264 for online platforms, and a colour-graded master for archive. A full EPK and stills package was produced alongside the film.
