The Challenge

A brand with strong product credentials but no coherent visual or digital presence. The brief was to build something that could hold its own next to the best in its category — without losing what made it distinct in the first place.

Identity & Digital

We began with a full identity audit before touching anything new. Understanding what existed — and why it had stopped working — was more valuable than arriving with a pre-formed direction. From that audit came a revised visual system: a tighter typographic palette, a disciplined colour approach, and a set of motion principles that carried through every touchpoint.

The digital platform was rebuilt from the ground up using the new identity as its foundation. Every interaction was considered against the brand's core proposition. The result is a site that feels like the brand, not like a template the brand was poured into.

Campaign Execution

A launch campaign was developed across digital, social, and selected print — built around a single clear message and executed without compromise across every format. Three months in, the brand's digital engagement had more than doubled.
