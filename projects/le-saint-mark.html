The Brief

Le Saint Mark needed a full campaign identity for the release of their debut project — something that could live across music, visuals, and digital simultaneously. The brief was clear: build a world, not just a rollout.

The Approach

We started with the sonic identity — working closely with the artist to understand the emotional register of the project — then reverse-engineered the visual language from there. Everything, from the typography to the colour palette to the camera movement in the music video, was built from a single creative brief.

The campaign spanned a music video, a series of stills, a digital identity package, and a release strategy built around phased drops that let each piece breathe before the next arrived.

The Result

A cohesive body of work that felt like a statement rather than a release. The campaign positioned Le Saint Mark as an artist with a fully formed point of view — which is the only kind of artist worth paying attention to.
