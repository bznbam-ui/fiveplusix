Le Saint Mark

Rooted in soulful storytelling and cinematic production. Le Saint Mark builds sound worlds that sit between classic R&B and something entirely new — music that earns its place in the room.

The Artist

Le Saint Mark arrived at FivePluSix with a clear voice and the discipline to develop it. The music sits at the intersection of classic soul structure and contemporary production — rooted enough to feel familiar, forward enough to feel current. The balance is deliberate and hard-won.

The name comes from a place and a posture: Saint, for the weight of intention behind every release; Mark, for the impression it leaves. Everything is built around that idea — music that marks something, that means something after the listening stops.

Sound

Production-heavy R&B built on live instrumentation and layered synthesis. Vocals that treat melody as architecture — structural, not decorative. References that span Marvin Gaye and James Blake without sounding like either. The goal, always, is to be unmistakable.

Releases

Campaign Sessions EP (2023), Saint's Gold single (2024), First Light album (2021). Full campaign visual produced by FivePluSix Film in 2024. Further releases in development.
