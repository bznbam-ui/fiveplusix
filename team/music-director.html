Head of Music · Producer
Music Director

A&R, production, and artist development. Responsible for the sonic identity of every FivePluSix release — from first session to final master.

Background

Over a decade of production credits across R&B, electronic, and experimental music — working with independent artists and label-signed acts before joining FivePluSix. Developed a production approach that starts with the artist's voice and works backward to the arrangement, rather than imposing a template and filling it in.

The work is detailed and unhurried. A track is finished when it's finished, not when the session runs out. That discipline is what separates a release that lasts from one that disappears in three months.

Role at FivePluSix

Leads all music production, A&R, and artist development across the label. First point of contact for all new artist signings and the primary creative collaborator in the studio. Also oversees sync licensing and placement strategy for all FivePluSix catalogue.

Selected Credits

LFA — Vol. I EP, production and mixing. Le Saint Mark — Saint's Gold, production. FivePluSix Collective — Untitled No. 7, producer. Multiple unreleased projects in development.
